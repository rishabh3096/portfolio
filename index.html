About

A visual and motion designer in London, I build brands and then set them in motion, from the first idea through launch campaigns, product films and the systems that hold it all together.

I spent nearly five years as Lead Visual Artist at boAt, leading launches and partnerships with Netflix, Warner Bros, Disney and Dolby. Since finishing my MA at London College of Communication, I’ve been freelancing, and I’m always open to the next good project.
