About

I’m Rishabh, a visual and motion designer in London. I help brands look sharp and move well.

For nearly five years I was Lead Visual Artist at boAt, India’s leading audio and wearables brand, shaping how it showed up across every touchpoint: launch campaigns, product films, social and partnerships with Netflix, Warner Bros, Disney and Dolby.

My work sits where brand identity meets motion. I work in Figma, After Effects and the Adobe Creative Suite, and bring AI into the workflow where it genuinely helps. I’m used to working async with global teams, presenting the thinking behind the work, and keeping several projects moving at once.

I hold an MA in Design for Visual Communication from London College of Communication, UAL. I’m now freelancing from London and open to new projects.
